Operational Research model

Decision: assign one feasible waiting vessel to each free berth when pilot, tug and weather constraints allow.

Feasibility: LOA ≤ berth length, draft ≤ berth depth − safety UKC margin, and cargo type compatible with terminal.

Objective: policy-dependent score combining waiting time, estimated service duration, vessel priority and delay cost.

Service time: maneuvering + mooring + cargo quantity ÷ effective crane rate, adjusted by vessel/berth match and weather.

Resources: pilots and tugs are seized during pilotage/berthing and released after all fast; cranes are allocated while alongside.

Educational simulation only — not for navigation, berth planning or real-world shiphandling decisions.
